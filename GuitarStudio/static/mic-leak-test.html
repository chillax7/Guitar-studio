What the result tells us

  1. Level 1 climbs — the leak is in Chrome's capture stack itself. Nothing in the app is involved; the fix is a workaround plus a Chrome bug report.
  2. Level 2 climbs — it is createMediaStreamSource feeding an AudioContext, i.e. still Chrome, but tied to how we open the context.
  3. Level 3 climbs — the meter loop's analyser reads are implicated.
  4. Level 4 climbs — an AudioWorklet in the live path is implicated, and that is ours to fix.
